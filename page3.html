Good drinks.

Café ITECH is a student-inspired café serving specialty coffees, creative refreshers, and drinks with a twist. We wanted a place where people can grab something different, study, or just hang out with friends.

Our beginnings

Our Story

Café ITECH started as an idea between three college students who wanted to create something different from the usual coffee shop. We love unique drinks, good coffee, and a place that feels welcoming. Our goal is to bring students and the community together one drink at a time.
